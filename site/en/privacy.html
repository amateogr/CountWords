Privacy

Privacy policy

Your text

Text you type or import (.txt, .docx, .pdf) is processed entirely in your browser, in a local Web Worker. It is never sent to any server — ours or anyone else's. There's no backend: close the tab and the text is gone without a trace.

Analytics

We use Plausible Analytics to measure aggregate traffic (page views, approximate country, browser type). Plausible doesn't use cookies, doesn't generate a unique per-visitor identifier, and can't be used to reconstruct your browsing history across sites. Full detail in their data policy.

Third parties that are loaded

Fonts are served from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.com); Google may log that request under its own policy. The import engines (mammoth.js, pdf.js) are served from this same domain, not from an external CDN — no network call is associated with them.

No accounts, no first-party cookies

This tool has no user accounts, no first-party cookies, and no persistent browser storage (no localStorage, no IndexedDB). Every visit starts from a blank slate.
